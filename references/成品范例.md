# 成品范例

这些是**格式范例**：演示字段分工、内容顺序、词组 / 句子的分配、附注怎么写。
tag 已用 `scripts/lookup_tag.py` 查证存在且有 post（2026-09），但范例本身**未经出图实测**，
出图效果以各分册的实测判据为准。照抄前仍按 `写法-收尾与排查.md` §9 检查表过一遍。

每条范例的共同点：人数 tag 最前且只出现一次；发型 / 眼睛 / 服装只在角色栏；
互斥组（视线、取景距离、背景形态、体位、水平机位）各只挑一个；姿势与机位用 tag，
空间关系 / 占画幅 / 进行中 / 光影叙事用句子且不加引号；没有质量尾；UC 只有两档；
画师串没有就留占位行；不复述流程。

## 范例 1 · A 档：需求已成型，直译

用户：「白发红眼的女孩坐在图书馆窗边看书，午后阳光，很安静」

谁（白发红眼女孩）、在哪（图书馆窗边）、什么调子（午后阳光、安静）都有 → A 档，
一条，不自作主张加内容，不写附注。

```
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
1girl, solo, library, indoors, window, bookshelf, day, upper body, from side, sitting, reading, holding book, sunlight

She sits at the reading desk right beside a tall window, the open book resting on the desk in front of her. Afternoon sunlight falls across the pages and her shoulder; the rows of shelves behind her stay dim, and nothing else in the room is moving.

Character 1:
girl, white hair, red eyes

UC:
用默认预设
```

要点：用户没给发型长短、服装，所以角色栏只有 `white hair, red eyes`，不补；
「安静」不是表情词，落在句子里（没有别的东西在动）。

## 范例 2 · B 档：只给了主体，补情景与镜头

用户：「画个猫耳少女」

只有「谁」→ B 档，从监督起步补情景镜头，缺的格全挑主力（单人、看镜头、大主体、
柔光、中低对比），一条，末尾一行说明补了哪几处。

```
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
1girl, solo, smile, looking at viewer, outdoors, street, day, blurry background, cowboy shot, straight-on, standing, hand up, sunlight, warm colors

She stands in the middle of a quiet side street facing the camera, one hand lifted in a small wave, and takes up most of the frame; the shopfronts behind her fall out of focus in soft, even afternoon light with no hard shadows.

Character 1:
girl, cat ears, cat tail, brown hair, medium hair, green eyes, white shirt, pleated skirt, school bag

UC:
用默认预设

补了：外貌细节（发色 / 发型 / 瞳色 / 服装）、街景、站姿与挥手、柔光暖色。
```

要点：`blurry background` 是背景形态那一组里唯一的一个；`straight-on` 是水平机位，
`cowboy shot` 是取景距离，各只一个；补的部分不抢戏，没有加动作戏或特殊光效。
光的软硬写在句子里——`soft lighting` 查证是空词条（0 post），`city street` 同样为空，
所以用 `sunlight` + `street`。

## 范例 3 · C 档：只给了方向，出三条不同取向

用户：「有没有夏天的图」

只有题材 → C 档。没有图片检索能力时先点明这是新写的提示词，然后给 2–3 条，
每条至少在两个层上不同，至少一条走空地，每条末尾一行取向说明。
这三条就是 `通用构思.md`「一个完整例子（C 档）」那张表的落笔。

这是新写的提示词，不是找现成的图。

```
方案 A · 黄昏逆光·河堤
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
1girl, solo, light smile, looking back, looking at viewer, outdoors, riverbank, river, summer, sunset, cowboy shot, from behind, walking, backlighting, warm colors

She is walking home along the top of the embankment and has just turned her head back toward the camera; the low sun sits behind her, rimming her hair and the edge of her skirt in warm light while her face stays in soft shadow. The river below carries a long orange reflection of the sky.

Character 1:
girl, cat ears, cat tail, black hair, long hair, brown eyes, serafuku, pleated skirt

UC:
用默认预设

按群基线走，只在光上偏——逆光。

方案 B · 室内午后·避暑
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
1girl, solo, sleepy, half-closed eyes, looking at viewer, indoors, wooden floor, electric fan, summer, day, close-up, from above, lying, on stomach, head rest, warm colors

She is lying face-down on the cool wooden floor with her cheek on her folded arms, looking up at the camera through half-closed eyes; an electric fan turns just out of frame so a few strands of hair lift and fall. Warm, diffused afternoon light fills the room evenly, with almost no hard shadows.

Character 1:
girl, cat ears, cat tail, black hair, long hair, brown eyes, white tank top, shorts

UC:
用默认预设

全部主力档，最保守的一条。

方案 C · 远景小人·正午海边
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
1girl, solo, looking afar, outdoors, beach, ocean, horizon, blue sky, summer, day, wide shot, full body, standing, sunlight, high contrast, shadow

She is a small figure standing at the waterline near the bottom-left of the frame, taking up only a sliver of the picture; the wide beach, the sea and a huge midday sky are the real subject. The noon sun is directly overhead, so her shadow is a short dark pool at her feet, the lit sand is almost white, and the cold blue of the sea sits against the hot pale sand.

Character 1:
girl, cat ears, cat tail, black hair, long hair, brown eyes, white sundress, straw hat

UC:
用默认预设

两处刻意偏离：主体占幅压到很小（群里 92% 是大主体）、硬光强对比（群里高对比只占 24%）。
不确定 tag：`high contrast`（仅 4.8k post，偏弱）——删掉的话对比强度全靠句子。
```

要点：三条的「有记忆点的那一下」各不相同（回头 / 趴着困倦 / 一个人面对海）；
A 与 B 差在动作、场景、镜头、调子，C 与两者差在题材、镜头、调子且走了远景小人 + 硬光
两块空地（这是唯一允许叠两块的位置）。角色栏每条各给一份，不共用。
`looking back` 不算视线方向，所以可以和 `looking at viewer` 叠；`wide shot` 可以和
`full body` 叠。取向说明只说偏离，不说流程。
硬光与投影形状由句子承担：`harsh lighting` / `cast shadow` 查证都不是 danbooru 词条，
`soft lighting` 是空词条——光的性质基本只能靠句子写，tag 只钉 `sunlight` / `backlighting` /
`high contrast` 这几个真有 post 的。

## 范例 4 · 双人交互：施受前缀写在角色栏

用户：「两个女孩在放学后的教室，一个逗另一个，被逗的那个脸红」

两个人 → 角色栏必须分开；「谁对谁做什么」容易错，所以施受方向用 `source#` / `target#`
钉在角色栏，句子用外观指认而不用编号；两人图 UC 不带 `extra characters`；
只有两人且没有前后景层次，不附 Position 行。

```
Prompt:
<画师串：自己贴>
2girls, classroom, indoors, evening, sunset, window, school desk, upper body, from side, sitting

The girl with black twintails leans across the desk and presses a fingertip against the pink-haired girl's cheek; the pink-haired girl has turned her face half away, shoulders drawn up, both of her hands still flat on her own desk. Low orange light comes in through the window on the left and catches the black-haired girl's face, while the pink-haired girl is mostly lit from behind. Each hand is clearly attached to its own owner.

Character 1:
girl, black hair, twintails, long hair, red eyes, serafuku, source#teasing, source#touching face, smirk, looking at target

Character 2:
girl, pink hair, short hair, blue eyes, serafuku, target#teased, target#face touched, blush, embarrassed

UC:
用默认预设，但去掉预设里 `extra characters` 那一段
```

要点：`smirk` / `blush` 是 §0.1 说的表情词唯一例外——挂着施受前缀时才允许进角色栏；
主提示词里不写视线 tag，视线由 Character 1 的 `looking at target` 管；
`sitting` 两人共用一个体位词属于多角色例外；人数 tag `2girls` 与角色栏恰好两格一致。
前缀后面接的 `touching face` / `face touched` / `teased` 和 `looking at target` 都**不是**
danbooru 词条（查证 0 post 或不存在）——它们是施受前缀接的自然语言短语，写法来自
§4.9 引用的真实语料；`teasing` 4.5k 是其中唯一的规范 tag。
